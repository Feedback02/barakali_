# Barakali — Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Barakali collects and uses your personal data, and your rights. It is designed to meet both the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan "On Personal Data" (No. ZRU-547).

## 2. What data we collect

- **Account data:** your mobile phone number, display name, and chosen language.
- **Role data:** whether you are a consumer, merchant, or administrator, and (for merchants) business details you provide.
- **Order data:** offers you reserve, amounts paid, pickup codes, pickup status, and ratings you leave.
- **Location data:** your approximate device location, only if you grant permission, to show nearby offers.
- **Device & notification data:** a push-notification token (if you enable notifications) and basic technical/diagnostic data.
- **Consent records:** a versioned, timestamped record of the consents you give.

We do **not** collect biometric, genetic, or special-category data.

## 3. Why we use your data and our legal basis

- To create and manage your account and provide the service — *performance of a contract*.
- To process reservations, payments, pickups, and refunds — *performance of a contract*.
- To show nearby offers using location — *your consent* (you can deny or revoke location permission).
- To send transactional notifications (e.g. order confirmed, pickup reminder) — *performance of a contract / legitimate interest*.
- To send marketing messages — *your consent only*, which you can withdraw at any time.
- To keep records, prevent fraud and abuse, and meet legal obligations — *legitimate interest / legal obligation*.

Providing account and order data is necessary to use Barakali; without it we cannot provide the service. Location and marketing are optional.

## 4. Who we share data with

- **Merchants** — the minimum needed to fulfill your order (e.g. that an order exists and its pickup code).
- **Service providers acting on our behalf:** our cloud/database host (Supabase, hosted in the EU), our SMS provider (Eskiz.uz) for verification codes, the push-notification service (Google Firebase Cloud Messaging), payment providers (Payme / Click) to process payments, and error/analytics tools.
- **Authorities** — where required by law.

We do not sell your personal data.

## 5. International data transfers

Barakali's servers are currently hosted in the European Union (AWS, EU region). Because you are in Uzbekistan, your data may be processed outside Uzbekistan. We rely on your explicit consent to cross-border transfer (given at registration) and on the conditions permitted under Uzbek law for storing non-sensitive personal data abroad (appropriate information-security measures, compliance with international data-protection standards, and oversight by the authorized state body). The EU provides a high standard of data protection.

## 6. How long we keep your data

- Order records are anonymized after 24 months.
- Inactive accounts are deleted after 12 months of inactivity.
- Consent records are kept as a versioned, append-only history for as long as required to demonstrate compliance.

When you delete your account, your personal data is erased or anonymized, except where we must keep limited records to comply with the law.

## 7. Your rights

You have the right to: access your data; correct inaccurate data; have your data erased; restrict or object to processing; data portability; and to withdraw any consent at any time (without affecting prior processing). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority — in Uzbekistan, the State Personalization Center; in the EU, your local data-protection authority.

You can exercise key rights directly in the app: **export your data** (a JSON copy) and **delete your account** from the Profile screen.

## 8. Marketing messages

We send marketing messages only if you opt in. You can withdraw consent at any time in notification settings or by contacting us; we will stop and update your consent record.

## 9. How we protect your data

We use access controls, encryption in transit, row-level security on our database, and least-privilege server-side processing for sensitive operations (such as payments). No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

## 10. Children

Barakali is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
